GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

More details about making Kelmora part of your next trip.

What can I do with Kelmora?+

Create and organize trips, plan itineraries, save places, manage travel notes and expenses, check destination weather, and request AI-generated trip suggestions.

Does Kelmora need my location?+

The current Android app does not request device location permission. You can enter a destination or search for places; those searches may be sent to the relevant service to return results.

How does the AI planner work?+

If you choose to generate a plan, the trip details you provide for that request—such as origin, destination, dates, budget, traveler count, travel style, and interests—are sent through the app’s service to generate suggestions.

Can I use Kelmora without notifications?+

Yes. Android notification permission is optional. If you allow it, the app can show reminders and budget or quick-add expense notifications.

Does Kelmora show ads?+

The app includes Google Mobile Ads. Google may process device and advertising information to provide and measure ads, subject to your consent choices and Google’s policies.
